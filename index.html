DataWarden privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026

DataWarden keeps everything on your device. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and the developer never receives your data.

What DataWarden does with your information

DataWarden measures your mobile data use and the type of network you are on (5G, 4G or 3G) so it can show your usage and warn you when your connection drops. All of this is calculated and stored on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any server the developer runs.

Information the app uses

  • Data usage: how much mobile data this device, your hotspot, USB sharing and Bluetooth sharing use, and (with usage access) how much each app uses.
  • Network details: your network type, signal strength and carrier name, and which SIM is active (this uses the phone-state permission).
  • Your plans and settings: plan names, limits, alert choices and your app PIN, if you set one.
  • Location, only if you turn on Places: where you were and which network you had there, so the app can show which places have better coverage. The location is precise (about 1 metre) and is stored only on your device.
  • Server details you enter for the VPN feature. Any keys or passwords are kept in your device's secure storage.

Location

Places is off until you turn it on, and the app explains what it records before asking for permission. It records location only while the app's background service is running. You can delete one list or all places at any time in Places, or turn Places off. DataWarden does not record your location when Places is off.

Sharing with your other device

If you turn on "Share with your other phone", DataWarden makes your plans, usage and network status available to devices on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot network. A device can read them only after it has been paired with the code shown on your phone. The connection stays inside your local network and is not encrypted, so use it only on networks you trust. "Show on a computer" works the same way and is off until you switch it on. You can turn either off at any time.

Sharing your connection with your devices

If you turn on "Share my VPN" (it is off until you switch it on), your own other devices can send their internet traffic through this phone, over your Wi-Fi or hotspot, using this phone's connection and its VPN if one is on. A device can do this only with the access code shown on your phone; any other device is refused. The traffic just passes through your phone to where it was going. DataWarden does not read it, keep it or send it to the developer; it only counts how much data passed, so the amount appears in your usage. The link between the device and your phone is not encrypted, so use it only on networks you trust. If you turn on "Pause sharing when the network drops", DataWarden stops carrying these connections while your phone is below your preferred network or a data limit is reached, and starts again by itself. You can turn sharing off at any time.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to the developer. The app connects to the internet only to (1) talk to your own devices on your local network, (2) run the VPN tunnels you set up, to the servers you chose, (3) pass on your own devices' traffic when you turn on sharing, and (4) open links you tap, such as your maps app for a saved place. DataWarden contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Storage and deletion

Everything is stored on your device and is not included in cloud backups. Places you have not returned to are removed automatically after a while. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it.

Permissions

Usage access shows how much data each app uses. Phone state identifies your SIM and carrier. Location is used only for Places. Notifications deliver your alerts. Running in the background and ignoring battery optimisation keep monitoring and alerts working. The VPN permission is used only when you start a VPN tunnel.

Children

DataWarden is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with a new effective date.
